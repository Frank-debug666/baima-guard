# Risk Pattern Reference

Use these categories when explaining a result:

- `money_request`: asks for transfer, payment, deposit, recharge, or a safe account.
- `secrecy`: asks the recipient not to tell others, report the message, or seek help.
- `urgency`: pressures the recipient to act immediately or within a short deadline.
- `impersonation`: claims to be an authority, manager, relative, or implausible identity.
- `lure`: promises prizes, rebates, high returns, or easy income.
- `privacy_theft`: requests verification codes, passwords, identity data, screen sharing, or remote access.

Treat combinations as stronger evidence than isolated keywords. Mention uncertainty and recommend independent verification.
